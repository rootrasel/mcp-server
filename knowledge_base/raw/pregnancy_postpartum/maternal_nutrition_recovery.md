# Maternal Wellness, Trimester Nutrition & Postpartum Recovery

## Journey: Pregnancy & Postpartum
**Category:** maternal_nutrition / symptom_education / postpartum_recovery

### Trimester-Specific Nutritional Foundations
Maternal nutritional needs adapt across gestation:
- **First Trimester:** Key focus is on adequate bioavailable folate (methylfolate or folinic acid), vitamin B6 for mild nausea management, and small, frequent meals containing balanced complex carbohydrates to stabilize blood sugar.
- **Second Trimester:** Blood volume expands by approximately 40–50%, increasing demand for elemental iron, vitamin C (to enhance iron absorption), and dietary choline (minimum 450 mg/day) to support fetal neural tube and hippocampal brain architecture.
- **Third Trimester:** Calcium and docosahexaenoic acid (DHA, minimum 200–300 mg/day) support rapid fetal skeletal mineralization and central nervous system development.

### Postpartum Fourth Trimester Pelvic & Hormonal Recovery
The postpartum phase represents an intensive period of tissue remodeling and endocrine re-equilibration. After placental delivery, estrogen and progesterone plunge dramatically. Restorative postpartum care centers on:
1. Warm, easily digestible nutrient-dense foods (bone broths, slow-cooked stews, healthy fats).
2. Gentle breath-synchronized pelvic floor activation (avoiding premature high-impact loading).
3. Collagen peptides, zinc, and amino acids to support perineal and abdominal connective tissue healing.

### Safety Red-Flags During Gestation and Postpartum
Expectant mothers must seek urgent medical evaluation for:
- Severe sudden headaches not relieved by acetaminophen.
- Visual disturbances (aura, flashing spots, sudden blurriness).
- Sudden swelling of the face, hands, or feet (signs of preeclampsia).
- Heavy vaginal bleeding (soaking more than one maxi pad per hour).
- Unilateral calf pain, tenderness, or swelling (risk of deep vein thrombosis).
