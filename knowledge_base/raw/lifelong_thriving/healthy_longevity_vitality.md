# Healthy Longevity, Cognitive Wellness & Vitality Scoring

## Journey: Lifelong Thriving
**Category:** healthy_aging / cognitive_wellness / mobility_joint_health

### Sarcopenia Prevention and Functional Mobility
In postmenopausal years, maintaining skeletal muscle mass and muscular power is the single strongest correlate with metabolic health, insulin sensitivity, and fall prevention. Sarcopenia (age-related loss of muscle mass) accelerates without targeted mechanical resistance exercise. Women should focus on compound multi-joint movements (squat patterns, hinge patterns, pushing, and pulling) combined with adequate dietary protein distributed across meals (30–40 grams per meal containing ~3 grams of leucine to stimulate mammalian target of rapamycin / mTOR).

### Cardiovascular and Endothelial Vitality
Following the menopausal transition, loss of endogenous estrogen protection alters the lipid profile and reduces nitric oxide-mediated arterial elasticity. Maintaining vascular health involves:
- Regular zone 2 cardiovascular endurance training (30–45 minutes, 3–4 days weekly).
- Nitrate-rich vegetables (arugula, beetroot) and polyphenol-dense berries to enhance endothelial nitric oxide synthase (eNOS) activity.
- Monitoring resting blood pressure, fasting ApoB, and high-sensitivity C-reactive protein (hs-CRP).

### Cognitive Vitality and Neuroprotection
Cognitive longevity is preserved through multi-domain lifestyle interventions: continuous lifelong learning, robust social engagement, deep restorative sleep for glymphatic clearance of neurotoxic proteins, and an anti-inflammatory Mediterranean-style dietary pattern rich in extra virgin olive oil, walnuts, and DHA.
